One list, everyone can add to it, and it updates on everyone's phone as it changes. The person at the supermarket sees what got added while they were walking there.

## Adding items

Type the item and add it. Set a quantity if it matters.

Everyone in the flat sees it immediately.

## Shopping

Tick items off as you put them in the trolley. They disappear from everyone else's list at the same time, so two of you do not come home with two bags of rice.

Tap **Clear bought** afterwards to tidy up. Your purchase history is kept separately, so clearing the list loses nothing.

## Suggestions

FlatApp notices what you buy repeatedly. Once an item has come up a few times it starts appearing as a suggestion when you go to add something, so the weekly staples take one tap instead of typing.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Pin an item** | Always suggest it, regardless of how often you buy it |
| **Dismiss a suggestion** | Stop suggesting it |

Suggestions are matched on the item's name, so **Milk** and **milk** count as two different things. Worth being consistent about spelling if your suggestions start looking duplicated.

## Multiple lists

Most flats need one. You can make more when you need to separate things out:

- **Groceries** for the weekly shop
- **Household** for cleaning supplies and light bulbs
- **Party** for a one-off

Rename, reorder, or delete lists as you like.

## Reordering

Drag items to reorder them. Grouping by aisle makes a big shop meaningfully faster.

## History

Every purchase is recorded - what, when, by whom. Useful for "did anyone actually buy dishwasher tablets last week", and it is what drives the suggestions.

## Tips

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Add things the moment you notice">
    The whole point is not having to remember until you are in the shop. Add it when you use the last of it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Be specific when it matters">
    "Bread" gets you whatever was nearest. "Sourdough" gets you sourdough.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Tick off as you go">
    Not when you get home. Someone else may be shopping at the same time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Log the shop as an expense">
    A shared shop is a shared cost. Add it on the [Expenses](/user-guide/expenses) tab while the receipt is in your hand.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
