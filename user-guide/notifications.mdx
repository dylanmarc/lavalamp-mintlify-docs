Notifications are what make FlatApp useful rather than another page you forget to open. Your chore reminders, payment confirmations and rent alerts all arrive this way.

## Turning them on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the app first">
    On iPhone this is required, not optional. See [Install the app](/user-guide/install-the-app).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings in FlatApp">
    Find the notifications section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable push notifications">
    Your phone asks for permission. Say yes - if you say no, you will have to re-enable FlatApp in your phone's own settings afterwards, which is fiddly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your categories">
    See below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Categories

Each can be turned on or off independently.

| Category | You get told about | On by default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🔔 **Reminders** | Upcoming reminders | Yes |
| ✅ **Chores** | Chores assigned to you, overdue, or due today | No |
| 💰 **Expenses** | New expenses and settlements | No |
| 📊 **Polls** | New polls to vote on | No |
| 📝 **Todos** | Todo updates | No |
| 🛒 **Shopping** | Shopping list updates | No |

<Tip>
  Only Reminders is on to begin with. If you want your rota to chase you - which is rather the point - turn on **Chores** as well. Most people find Chores, Expenses and Reminders is the right set, and leave Shopping and Todos off so the list does not buzz all day.
</Tip>

## What chore notifications look like

| When | Message |
| --- | --- |
| A chore becomes yours | "Chore assigned" |
| It is due tomorrow | "Due today: Kitchen" the morning before |
| Still not done on the day | A nudge at 5pm |
| It is past its due date | "Chore overdue" |

Morning notifications arrive from 9am in your flat's timezone. If your flat's timezone is wrong, these will land at strange hours - an admin can fix it in **Settings**.

## Your devices

Each phone, tablet or computer you enable notifications on is listed in **Settings**. Remove any you no longer use with **Remove device**.

## The notification bell

The bell in the app holds everything FlatApp has sent you, whether or not push reached your device. If you have push switched off, this is still a complete record. The badge shows how many you have not read.

## Nothing is arriving

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="On iPhone">
    Open FlatApp from the home screen icon, not from a Safari tab. Push does not work in the browser on iOS. Also check **Settings → Notifications → FlatApp** on the phone itself.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Only some kinds are missing">
    That category is switched off. Most are off by default. Check the list in FlatApp's **Settings**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="They arrive at odd hours">
    The flat's timezone is wrong. An admin can change it under **Settings → Flat timezone**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It worked and then stopped">
    Phones occasionally drop the connection. Turn notifications off and back on in FlatApp's settings to re-register the device.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Nothing at all, on any device">
    Contact support and mention when it last worked. See [Getting help](/user-guide/account/support).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
