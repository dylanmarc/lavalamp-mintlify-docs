Chores are the reason most flats install FlatApp. Set the rota up once and it moves itself along, chases whoever's turn it is, and keeps a record so nobody has to argue from memory.

## Adding a chore

Tap the **+** button on the **Chores** screen.

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | "Kitchen", "Bins", "Bathroom" |
| **Description** | Optional. What "done" actually means, which saves a lot of grief |
| **Difficulty** | Easy, medium or hard. Sets the XP and coins it is worth |
| **Due date** | When it should be finished |
| **Repeats** | Daily, weekly, monthly, or not at all |
| **Assigned to** | Who is doing it |
| **Rotation** | Turn this on to share it around |

<Tip>
  Be specific in the description. "Kitchen" means different things to different people; "wipe benches, empty bin, run and empty the dishwasher" does not.
</Tip>

## Rotation

Rotation is what makes this worth setting up. Turn it on, pick the people, and put them in order. FlatApp assigns the chore to the first person, and when the period is up it moves to the next one on its own.

For a weekly chore you can pin which day it changes over - Sunday night is a common choice, so the new week starts with a clean slate.

<Note>
  Adding someone to a rotation restarts the order from the top rather than slotting them in at the end. Worth knowing before you add a new flatmate mid-cycle.
</Note>

### The default rotation

An admin can set a default order for the whole flat in **Settings**, and **Apply to all chores** pushes it onto every rotating chore at once. Useful when someone moves in or out and you would otherwise be editing every chore by hand.

## Completing a chore

Tap **Complete** on your chore. You earn XP and coins, and it is recorded against your name.

Only the person it is assigned to can mark it done. Doing someone else's chore for them is generous but FlatApp will not credit you for it.

Completing a chore does not immediately pass it on - it stays yours until the rota turns over. So you can do the kitchen on Tuesday without accidentally handing the rest of your week to the next person.

## Running late

Life happens. FlatApp records it without being unpleasant about it.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Done on time | Full XP and coins |
| Done late | XP reduced 20% per day late, down to a floor of 20%. Coins are not reduced |
| Not done before the rota turns over | Recorded as missed |

A missed chore can still be completed afterwards. Open the chore's **History**, find the missed entry with your name on it, and tap **Complete Late**. It shows as completed late rather than missed, which is a more honest record than either pretending it was on time or leaving it as a miss.

## History

Every chore keeps a record: who was responsible for each period, whether it was done, on time, late, or missed, and the points earned. **Chores → History** shows the whole flat.

This is the feature that settles arguments. It is also worth looking at occasionally when nobody is arguing - an uneven split usually shows up here before anyone says anything.

## Reminders you will get

With **Chores** notifications enabled:

| When | |
| --- | --- |
| A chore becomes yours | "Chore assigned" |
| The morning before it is due | "Due today" |
| 5pm on the day, if still not done | A nudge |
| After the due date | "Chore overdue" |

Morning notifications arrive from 9am in your flat's timezone.

## Tips

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Start small">
    Three or four rotating chores that actually get done beats fifteen that everyone ignores within a fortnight.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Match difficulty to effort">
    If the bathroom takes an hour and the bins take two minutes, marking them both medium will make the rewards feel arbitrary and people will stop caring about them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Change over on a Sunday night">
    A weekly rota that turns over mid-week makes it much harder to remember whose turn it is.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Use Complete Late honestly">
    It exists so the record is true. A flat where missed chores get quietly deleted is a flat where the history is worthless.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
