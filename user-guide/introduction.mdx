FlatApp keeps a shared household running without anyone having to be the person who nags. Everyone in your flat sees the same chores, the same balances, and the same shopping list, updated live.

## What you can do

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Chores** | Share out the cleaning on a rota that takes its own turn each week, with a history of who actually did what |
| **Expenses** | Log what you paid, see who owes who, and settle up with both sides confirming |
| **Shopping** | One live list everyone can add to, that learns what you buy regularly |
| **Todos** | Shared task lists for things that are not recurring chores |
| **Reminders** | Rent day, bin night, the inspection - one-off or repeating |
| **Polls** | Settle a decision without a group chat argument |
| **Rewards** | XP, levels, coins and collectable profile cosmetics for getting things done |

## How it fits together

<Steps>
  <Step title="One person creates the flat">
    They get an invite link to share.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Everyone else joins with that link">
    Tap it, sign up, and you are in. No codes to read out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your chores and reminders">
    Add the recurring things once. The rota moves itself from then on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on notifications">
    Otherwise FlatApp is a page you have to remember to open, which rather defeats the point.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Everything is shared with your flat">
    Every chore, expense, list and poll is visible to everyone in the flat. There is no private content. People outside your flat see nothing at all.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Updates are live">
    When someone ticks off the milk, it disappears from your list too, without a refresh.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="You can be in more than one flat">
    Useful if you are moving, or you share a holiday house. You switch between them; you are only ever looking at one at a time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It works offline, sort of">
    The app opens without a connection, but you need one to see or change anything. There is no offline queue.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
