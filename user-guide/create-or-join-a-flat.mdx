A **flat** is your household in FlatApp. Everything else sits inside it, so this comes first.

## Join a flat you have been invited to

The easiest path. Someone in the flat sends you a link.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap the invite link">
    It looks like `getflatapp.com/join?token=...`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign up or log in">
    If you do not have an account yet, create one. Your invite is remembered while you do this, so you will not need the link again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You are in">
    FlatApp drops you straight into the flat's dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you already have an account and are logged in, tapping the link joins you immediately.
</Tip>

### Or use the code

If the link will not work, ask for the flat's short code and enter it on the **Flats** screen. Codes deliberately leave out characters that are easy to confuse, so there is no `0` or `O` and no `1` or `I` in one.

## Create a new flat

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Email and password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Create a flat">
    Name it something everyone will recognise. "Wellington St" beats "My Flat".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your timezone">
    Under **Settings**. This decides when rotas change over and when reminders arrive. Get it right before you add chores.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite everyone">
    **Settings → New link**, then share it however you normally talk to each other.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Whoever creates the flat is its admin.

## Inviting people

From **Settings**, as an admin:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **New link** | Creates an invite link and copies it |
| **Copy short code** | The typeable version of the same invite |
| **Revoke invite** | Kills that link. People who already joined stay |

<Note>
  You can only copy an invite link at the moment you create it. FlatApp does not store the link itself, only enough to check it when someone uses it, which is what stops a leaked database handing out access to your flat. Lost the link? Revoke it and make a new one.
</Note>

## Being in more than one flat

You can belong to several flats - handy when you are moving out of one and into another. Use the flat switcher to change which one you are looking at. Your XP, coins and cosmetics follow you everywhere; chores, expenses and lists belong to each flat separately.

## Leaving a flat

**Settings → Leave Flat.**

You stop seeing the flat's chores, expenses and lists, and you are taken off any rotas. What you did while you were there stays in the flat's history, so balances and completion records still make sense for everyone else.

<Warning>
  Leaving does not settle what you owe. Sort out balances on the **Expenses** tab first, or you will leave people chasing you outside the app.
</Warning>

You can rejoin later with a fresh invite.
