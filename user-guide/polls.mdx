Ask the flat a question, give them options, and let everyone vote in their own time.

## Creating a poll

Tap **Create Poll**, write the question, and add the options.

Everyone in the flat is notified - except you, since you already know - if they have **Polls** notifications switched on.

## Voting

Open the poll and tap your choice. Results show as percentages and update live as people vote.

You can change your vote while the poll is open.

## Results

Everyone sees the running total, including who voted for what. Polls are not secret ballots. For most flat decisions this is a feature - "who actually wants the sofa" is more useful when you know who.

## Deleting

The person who created a poll can delete it. Votes go with it.

<Note>
  Poll options cannot be edited after the poll is created, because the votes are tied to the option as written. If you need to change the wording, delete it and start again.
</Note>

## Good uses

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Decisions with real options** | "What are we doing about the couch?" |
| **Scheduling** | "Which weekend suits for the flat dinner?" |
| **Spending** | "Do we split a new vacuum?" |
| **House rules** | "Quiet hours from 10 or 11?" |

## Less good uses

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Things one person can just decide">
    A poll on which brand of dish soap is not a decision, it is a delay.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Conflict">
    A vote will not resolve a disagreement about someone's behaviour, and putting it to the flat tends to make it worse. Have the conversation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Anything that needs a private answer">
    Everyone can see who voted for what.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
