The dashboard is where FlatApp opens: what you owe, what is coming up, and today's bonus.

## What is on it

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Expense summary">
    Your net position. One number for whether you are owed money or owe it, with a breakdown per flatmate. Tap through to [Expenses](/user-guide/expenses) to settle up.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Upcoming chores">
    What is assigned to you and when it is due, in your flat's timezone.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Chore timeline">
    A chart of who has completed what over recent weeks. Not a scoreboard so much as a way of noticing when the split has quietly become uneven.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Upcoming reminders">
    What is due soon - rent, bin night, an inspection.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Daily bonus">
    One spin a day for coins, and occasionally a reward case. See [Bounties and the daily bonus](/user-guide/rewards/bounties-and-daily-bonus).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Getting around

| Section | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | This page |
| **Reminders** | Dates that matter |
| **Chores** | The rota |
| **Expenses** | Money |
| **Polls** | Decisions |
| **Shopping** | The shared list |
| **Todos** | One-off tasks |
| **Settings** | Your profile, the flat, notifications |

On a phone, the main sections sit in the bottom bar; the rest are behind the menu. A badge on **Expenses** means someone is waiting for you to confirm a payment.

## Switching flats

If you are in more than one flat, use the switcher. The dashboard, and everything else, then shows only that flat.
