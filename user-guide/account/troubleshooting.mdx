## Notifications

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Nothing arrives on my iPhone">
    Push only works when FlatApp is installed to your home screen and opened from that icon, not from a Safari tab. See [Install the app](/user-guide/install-the-app). Then check **Settings → Notifications → FlatApp** on the phone itself.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Only some kinds arrive">
    Most categories are off by default. Only **Reminders** is on to start with. Turn on the ones you want in FlatApp's **Settings**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="They come at strange times">
    The flat's timezone is wrong. An admin changes it under **Settings → Flat timezone**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It worked and then stopped">
    Turn notifications off and back on in FlatApp's settings. That re-registers the device, which is usually all that is needed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Nothing on any device, for days">
    Contact [support](/user-guide/account/support) and say when it last worked.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Chores

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The rota is not moving">
    Check the chore actually has rotation switched on with people in the order, and that it is set to repeat. A chore with rotation off stays with whoever it was given to.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It changed over on the wrong day">
    Rotation follows the flat's timezone. Check it in **Settings**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I cannot complete a chore">
    Only the person it is assigned to can mark it done.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A chore shows as missed but I did it">
    Open the chore's **History**, find the missed entry with your name, and tap **Complete Late**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Adding someone restarted the order">
    That is how it works - changing who is in a rotation starts the order again from the top.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Expenses

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The split looks wrong">
    **Split with** is the *other* people. You are included automatically. Listing yourself as well makes it split one way too many.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An expense will not settle">
    Both sides have to act: the payer records the settlement, the payee confirms it. Check whether the other person has confirmed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I confirmed but it is still showing">
    Some expenses have several people settling. It is fully settled only when everyone involved has confirmed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I settled the wrong thing">
    Settlements cannot be deleted. Add a correcting expense with a note explaining it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A balance is a cent out">
    Some amounts do not divide evenly. FlatApp rounds consistently so your overall balance stays right.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The app itself

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Stuck loading">
    Close it completely and reopen. If that does not work, clear the site data for FlatApp in your browser settings and sign in again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Changes are not showing up">
    Check your connection. FlatApp needs one to read or write anything.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Something looks broken after an update">
    Fully close the app and reopen it so the new version loads properly.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I cannot see my flat">
    Check the flat switcher - you may be looking at a different one. If you have left the flat, ask for a new invite.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An invite link does not work">
    Links expire and can be revoked. Ask for a fresh one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Signing in

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No password reset email">
    Check spam, and check you used the address you signed up with.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Signed out unexpectedly">
    Sign back in. Clearing browser data signs you out.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Only the flat admin can change this">
    You are not the admin of that flat. Ask whoever created it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
