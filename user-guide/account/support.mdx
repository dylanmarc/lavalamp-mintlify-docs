## What to include

The more of this you send, the faster it gets sorted:

<Steps>
  <Step title="What you were doing">
    "Trying to confirm a payment from my flatmate."
  </Step>
  <Step title="What you expected">
    "The pending banner should have gone away."
  </Step>
  <Step title="What happened instead">
    "It is still there and says pending."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your device and browser">
    "iPhone 14, installed to home screen." Or "Chrome on Android."
  </Step>
  <Step title="When it started">
    "Since yesterday", or "the first time I tried it."
  </Step>
</Steps>

A screenshot usually saves a round of questions.

<Warning>
  Never include your password. Nobody from FlatApp will ever ask for it.
</Warning>
