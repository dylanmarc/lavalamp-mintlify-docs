## Who can see what

| | Who sees it |
| --- | --- |
| Your name, avatar, level, badges | Everyone in your flats |
| Chores, expenses, lists, reminders, polls | Everyone in that flat |
| Your email address | Only you |
| Your coins and inventory | Only you |
| Anything at all | Nobody outside your flats |

Flats are completely separate. Someone in one of your flats cannot see anything from another.

## Within a flat, nothing is private

Every chore, expense, list item, reminder and poll is visible to everyone in the flat. There is no private note and no hidden expense.

Polls are not secret ballots - everyone can see who voted for what.

## Notifications

FlatApp sends push notifications through your device's own notification service. Enabling them registers that device; the notification content goes through that service to reach your phone.

Turn them off at any time in **Settings**, or remove individual devices.

## Payments

FlatApp does not move money. You pay each other however you normally do, and FlatApp records that it happened. It never sees your bank details.

If your flat has a paid subscription, card details are handled entirely by Stripe and never touch FlatApp.

## Leaving a flat

What you did stays with the flat - completed chores, expenses you were part of, purchases you logged. Removing it would break everyone else's balances and history.

Your profile, level, badges and inventory go with you.

## Deleting your account

**Settings → Delete Account**, or [flatapp.com/delete-account](https://getflatapp.com/delete-account).

This removes your account, profile, XP, badges and inventory, permanently and immediately.

<Warning>
  It cannot be undone. Settle any outstanding balances first - deleting your account does not cancel what you owe, it just makes it harder for everyone to work out what that was.
</Warning>
