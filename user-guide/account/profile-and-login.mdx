## Signing up

Email and password. One account works across every flat you are in.

If you got here from an invite link, your invite is remembered while you sign up, so you go straight into the flat afterwards.

## Signing in

Email and password. You stay signed in on that device until you sign out, so day to day you just open the app.

## Forgotten password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Forgot password on the sign-in screen">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    A reset link is sent to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the link and set a new password">
    Then sign in with it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  No email after a few minutes? Check spam, and check you used the address you signed up with.
</Tip>

## Changing your details

**Settings**:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Your name** | What your flat sees |
| **Avatar** | Your picture |
| **Theme** | Light, dark, or follow your device |

Your name has to be at least two characters.

## Several flats

One account, as many flats as you need. Use the switcher to change which one you are looking at.

Your level, XP, badges, coins and inventory are yours and follow you between flats. Chores, expenses and lists belong to each flat.

## Devices

Every device where you have enabled notifications is listed in **Settings**. Remove old phones so they stop receiving things.

Signing in on a new device does not sign you out of the old one.

## Signing out

**Settings → Sign out.** Your data stays; you just log out of that device.

## Closing your account

**Settings → Delete Account**, or [flatapp.com/delete-account](https://getflatapp.com/delete-account).

<Warning>
  Deleting your account is permanent. Your profile, XP, badges and inventory are gone and cannot be restored. Settle up and leave your flats first.
</Warning>

See [Privacy and your data](/user-guide/account/privacy-and-data).
