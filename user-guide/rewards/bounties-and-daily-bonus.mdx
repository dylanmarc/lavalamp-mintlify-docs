## The daily bonus

One spin a day, free. It lands somewhere between 5 and 200 coins, and occasionally a reward case.

| Outcome | Chance |
| --- | --- |
| 5 coins | 40% |
| 10 coins | 25% |
| 25 coins | 15% |
| 50 coins | 10% |
| 100 coins | 5% |
| 200 coins | 2% |
| 25 coins + 1 case | 2.5% |
| 50 coins + 10 cases | 0.5% |

That last one is rare and worth waiting for.

<Tip>
  Roughly 26 coins a day on average, so a case every four days for opening the app. It resets at midnight in your flat's timezone and does not stack - miss a day and that spin is gone.
</Tip>

Claim it on the dashboard.

## Bounties

Bounties are optional extras your flat sets up itself - small jobs that are not worth a place on the rota but are worth doing.

- Take the recycling out
- Wipe down the bench
- Water the plants
- Bring the washing in

Anyone can create them, anyone can claim them, and each one can be claimed once per person per day. They reset daily, so a bounty is an ongoing standing offer rather than a one-off.

### What they pay

| Difficulty | XP | Coins |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Easy | 1 | 5 |
| Medium | 2 | 10 |
| Hard | 3 | 15 |

The XP is deliberately tiny. Bounties are defined by your own flat, so if they paid properly you could invent an easy one and mint XP off it. The coins are more generous because coins only buy cosmetics.

### Setting good ones

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Make them genuinely small">
    Two minutes. If it takes longer, it should be a chore or a todo.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Make them repeatable">
    Bounties reset every day, so they suit things that need doing often rather than once.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do not duplicate the rota">
    A bounty for something already on the rota just pays someone twice for one job.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Keep the list short">
    Five good bounties get claimed. Twenty get scrolled past.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Claim them from your profile.
