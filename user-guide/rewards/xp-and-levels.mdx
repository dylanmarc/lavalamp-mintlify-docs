Doing chores earns XP. XP raises your level, and your level shows next to your name.

## Earning XP

| | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Easy chore | 50 |
| Medium chore | 100 |
| Hard chore | 200 |
| Bounty | 1 to 3 |

Bounties are worth very little XP on purpose - they are a small extra, not a shortcut past the actual chores. They pay better in [coins](/user-guide/rewards/coins-and-cases).

## Being late

Finish late and the XP drops by 20% for each day:

| Days late | You get |
| --- | --- |
| On time | 100% |
| 1 day | 80% |
| 2 days | 60% |
| 3 days | 40% |
| 4 or more | 20% |

It stops at 20%, so a chore done very late is still worth something. Coins are not reduced for lateness.

## Your XP follows you

XP is yours, not the flat's. Move to a new flat and you keep your level, your badges and everything in your inventory. Only chores, expenses and lists belong to the flat.

## Where to see it

- **Your profile** - level, progress to the next one, total XP, recent activity
- **Next to your name** - level and any equipped title, everywhere in the app
- **Dashboard** - the chore timeline shows who has been doing what

<Tip>
  The fastest way up is finishing chores on time. A hard chore done on time is worth 200 XP; the same chore four days late is worth 40. Lateness costs more than difficulty pays.
</Tip>
