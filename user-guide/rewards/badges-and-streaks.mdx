## Badges

Badges are permanent. Earn one and it stays on your profile.

### Streak badges

For consecutive weeks of keeping on top of your chores.

| Badge | |
| --- | --- |
| 🔥 Getting Started | 1 week |
| 🔥 On a Roll | 2 weeks |
| 🔥 Hot Streak | 4 weeks |
| 🔥 Unstoppable | 8 weeks |
| 🔥 Legendary | 12 weeks |

### XP badges

| Badge | |
| --- | --- |
| ⭐ Beginner | 100 XP |
| ⭐ Contributor | 500 XP |
| ⭐ Dedicated | 1,000 XP |
| 🏆 XP Master | 2,500 XP |
| 🏆 XP Legend | 5,000 XP |

### Milestone badges

| Badge | |
| --- | --- |
| 👣 First Steps | First chore completed |
| 🎯 Half Century | 50 chores |
| 💯 Centurion | 100 chores |

There are bounty badges as well, for regular bounty claimers.

## Streaks

Your streak counts consecutive weeks in which you kept up with the recurring chores assigned to you.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Keeps going** | You finished the recurring chores that were yours that week |
| **Breaks** | You left recurring chores assigned to you unfinished |
| **Unaffected** | A week where nothing was assigned to you |

<Note>
  A week where you had no chores does not break your streak. Being away when you were not on the rota should not cost you eight weeks of work, so FlatApp does not count it against you.
</Note>

Streaks are counted per flat, and checked once a week.

## Where to see them

Your profile shows your badges, your current streak, your level and progress, and recent activity. Tap a flatmate's name anywhere in the app to see theirs.

## What this is for

None of it unlocks anything. It exists because the flats that keep on top of chores tend to be the ones where doing your share is visible, and a streak nobody wants to break turns out to be more effective than a rota nobody looks at.

<Tip>
  If your streak is about to break because of one chore, [Complete Late](/user-guide/chores#running-late) is still worth doing. The XP is reduced but the record is honest, and honest records are what make the whole thing work.
</Tip>
