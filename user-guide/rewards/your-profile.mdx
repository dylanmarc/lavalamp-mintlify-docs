Your profile is the summary of what you have done and how you look to everyone else.

## What is on it

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Name and avatar** | With whatever cosmetics you have equipped |
| **Level** | Number, title and colour |
| **Progress** | How far to the next level |
| **Badges** | Everything you have earned |
| **Streak** | Current run of weeks |
| **Recent activity** | Chores completed recently |
| **Cases** | Open them, or buy more |
| **Daily bounty** | Claim today's |

Tap any flatmate's name to see theirs.

## Customising

Five slots, one item each:

| Slot | |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | Text next to your name |
| **Colour** | Avatar background |
| **Frame** | Ring around your avatar |
| **Effect** | Animated flourish |
| **Emoji** | Small badge on your avatar |

Cosmetics come from [reward cases](/user-guide/rewards/coins-and-cases). Equip and unequip from your inventory as often as you like; nothing is used up by wearing it.

## Your inventory

Everything you own, grouped by kind and rarity. From here you can:

- **Equip** an item
- **Unequip** a slot
- **Scrap** a duplicate for coins

<Warning>
  Scrapping is permanent. Make sure you have a spare before scrapping anything you want to keep wearing.
</Warning>

**View Case Contents** shows the full catalogue, including what you have not got, which is useful for working out whether that legendary frame is worth chasing.

## Your name and photo

Change your display name and avatar in **Settings**. This is what your flat sees.

## Across flats

Your profile is yours, not the flat's. Level, XP, badges, coins and inventory move with you.

Streaks are counted per flat, because they are about keeping up with that flat's rota.
