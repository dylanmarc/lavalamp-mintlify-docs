Log what you paid, FlatApp works out who owes what, and settling up needs both people to confirm - so the record everyone is looking at is one everyone has agreed to.

## Adding an expense

Tap **+** on the **Expenses** screen.

| Field | |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | "Groceries", "Power bill", "Wifi" |
| **Amount** | The total paid |
| **Paid by** | Who actually paid. Can be someone other than you |
| **Split with** | Who else is sharing it |
| **Category** | For filtering later |
| **Notes** | Optional |

<Warning>
  **"Split with" means the other people, not everyone.** A $30 shop you paid for, split with two flatmates, is $10 each across all three of you - you are automatically included. Adding yourself to the list makes it a four-way split and the numbers will be wrong.
</Warning>

## Even and itemised splits

**Even** divides the total equally across you and everyone in **Split with**.

**Itemised** lets you set a different amount per person, for the supermarket trip where one person's half was mostly beer. Switch it on and enter each person's share; FlatApp checks the parts add up to the total.

## Balances

The **Balances** tab shows where you stand.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **You owe** | Total you owe others |
| **You are owed** | Total others owe you |
| **Net balance** | The single number that matters |
| **Per person** | Broken down by flatmate |

Where you both owe each other, FlatApp nets it off. You owe Sam $40 and Sam owes you $15, so it shows $25 and you make one transfer instead of two.

## Settling up

Money moves outside FlatApp - bank transfer, cash, whatever you use. FlatApp records that it happened, and it takes both of you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pay them for real">
    Bank transfer or cash. FlatApp does not move money.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark it in the app">
    **Balances** → select what you are settling → **Settle Selected**. Add a note if it helps ("bank transfer Tuesday").
  </Step>
  <Step title="They confirm">
    A **Pending Confirmations** banner appears at the top of their Expenses tab. They tap **Confirm Payment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    Once everyone involved has confirmed, the expense is marked settled for good.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The two-sided confirmation is deliberate. Only one person can see the money arrive, so only they can confirm it did. It also means nobody can quietly mark a debt as paid.
</Note>

### Confirming a payment

When someone pays you, the banner appears at the top of **Expenses** and the **Expenses** menu item gets a badge. Tap **Confirm Payment** on each. You can confirm several at once.

Everyone's view updates live, so the person who paid sees your confirmation as it happens.

## Finding things

Filter by category, by who paid, and by settled or unsettled. You only ever see expenses you are actually part of.

## Questions people ask

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Someone paid but has not marked it">
    Ask them to record it in FlatApp. You cannot confirm a payment that was never submitted.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I made a mistake on a settlement">
    Settlements cannot be deleted - that is what makes the history trustworthy. Add a correcting expense instead, with a note explaining it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The split is a cent out">
    Splitting $10 three ways cannot come out even. FlatApp handles the rounding consistently so balances stay correct across many expenses, even when a single one looks a cent off.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Someone is leaving and still owes money">
    Settle before they leave. Leaving a flat does not clear a balance, and chasing it afterwards is much harder.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I log an expense someone else paid?">
    Yes. Set **Paid by** to them. FlatApp records that you entered it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
